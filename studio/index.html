Studio

Pick a beat, record over it, stack your ad-libs, go back and fix a part, then save your song. It all runs on your phone. Your voice never leaves it.

Before your first take

  • Wear headphones. Without them the beat plays out loud and gets into your vocal.
  • Wired is best. Bluetooth earbuds hear the beat a little late. Using them anyway? Tap Sync my headphones in step 3 once and the page fixes the delay for you.
  • When your phone asks for the mic, tap Allow.
