# The fleet: ships by type, the first raider class, raiders in the shared world (2026-10-01)

Jaron: "the Meridian was the proof; now a fleet." Branch `cosmos-fleet` (from origin/main f4364e4, the live multiplayer). Not pushed.

## What was built

* **A ship is a type.** `ship.type` selects a definition from `src/ships/registry.js`; the Meridian is one entry (built from `shipSpec.js`, which stays the one place its numbers live) and the Shrike raider is another, in its own folder `src/ships/raider/` (spec, exterior, interior dressing, props, crew, brain, escorts, stats). The shared engine takes the definition instead of naming a ship: `ShipBody`, `GunSystem`, `Stations`, `ShipWalker` (`shipIndexFor`), the interior builder (kind-based, with a `custom` hook for a ship's own dressing), `ShipSystem` (`def`, `roles`, `visuals`), `CrewSystem`, the crew's routes, the server's board / leave / seat / hire / fire-gun / ramp rules, the multiplayer view. Every shared edit is marked `FLEET`.
* **The Shrike**: 36 m by 18 m (the Meridian is 49 by 25), 15.5 t. Rooms you walk between: cockpit, main corridor, crew quarters, mess, airlock (with a gangway), armoury, ladder niche to the dorsal turret, engine room (reactor, engineer's station), cargo hold with the stern ramp. Built by the same Kit, textures and depth-layer solver as the Meridian (51.7k interior triangles, 94 draw calls, phone budget is 100k / 220). Four stations (captain, pilot, engineer, dorsal turret), nose guns and a dorsal turret, four legs, two main engines with flames, four lift pods, an aft ramp and a port gangway.
* **Its crew**: four named people from the Loft's seven, seated at the four stations, flying and fighting it (`brain.js` flies it with the same three control numbers a stick writes; the gunners are the same `GunnerAI` hired crew use).
* **Raiders in the shared world** (`server/fleet.mjs`): one high over the port, one over Phobos, one abandoned hull adrift near Deimos; never in neutral airspace; they engage only a crewed ship in the air outside Mars's 1500 m line and not on a drive transit; strafing runs with the nose guns and the turret, three **escort drones** on their quarters (the old per-ship drones are these now), a break away after each pass, retreat when hurt, **disabled** at 35% hull (crew surrender), **abandoned** at none. Damage goes both ways through the same bolts (`fleet.mjs` explains the proxy spheres). Hired gunners on a player's ship defend it against raiders and escorts, and leave a surrendered raider alone.
* **Players can take one**: `claim-ship` (capture a disabled raider: its crew sign on, on wages; claim an abandoned one: empty), `buy-ship` at a kiosk by the hiring board (Shrike 2,400 credits), `set-flagship` (the page reloads into the new class). Each hull you own keeps its own pad, account and crew.
* **Client** (`src/world-state/fleetView.js`): every other ship is built from its own definition and posed from the server's record (gear, ramps, guns, engine glow, smoke below 55% hull), carried forward by its velocity between snapshots; raiders carry a label (name, state, hull); their crew are drawn seated; escorts and the fleet's bolts are drawn; hits on your own ship ripple the shield from the side of the nearest enemy; the World / crew panel has the fleet, the shipyard and a Capture / Claim button when you are alongside.

## What does not work, and what is not verified

* **Raiders exist only in the shared world.** The offline solo world keeps its three old drones; `?ship=raider` lets solo fly a Shrike but nothing attacks it. (The fleet is the server's; solo is the fallback.)
* **A captured ship is brought home by a prize crew** (it appears on a new pad at the port); you cannot fly it back from Phobos yourself, and the flagship change needs you on foot at the port.
* **Balance is by feel, not play-tested.** A raider is stronger than the old drones by design (about 535 damage to disable, nose guns 14 x 3.2/s, turret 9 x 3/s, three escorts at 12 per shot); an undefended Meridian in hostile air lasts about a minute. The sense range, the pass timing and the prices are mine and small. Raiders never shoot a ship in neutral airspace, so the neutral line is the whole safety rule. Phobos arrivals descend through the raider's patrol (that is the point; it is also a change to the job).
* **Seven faces.** The Loft has seven people; a raider's crew shares faces with the hiring pool. Names differ per boat.
* **Not measured on a phone.** All pictures are software GL in desktop Chromium. The phone build of the shared world (two skinned crews, escorts, remote ships built per type) is untested on a device: a remote ship's exterior is built once on first sight (13-14 ms here, measured in the browser run), people are drawn only within 700 m.
* **Server cost.** Raiders are always in the air, so the authority ticks at 30 Hz even with nobody on (before it idled at 1 Hz). Three raiders add about 4 ms per simulated second here; a restart after a long absence leaves them where they were (a 5 minute absence loads in about 2 s). Every landed idle ship still costs its landing physics every tick, as before. A capacity test with many players has not been run.
* **Public state grew** by about 17 kB (three raiders); it is broadcast ten times a second as before.
* **No ship-to-ship collision**, no boarding another ship in flight, no player-to-player ship trade.
* **Looks**: the Shrike reads as a different, smaller, meaner boat but shares the Meridian's textures and palette; the hull is only moderately darker. A modeller would give it its own panel set.
* Supabase persistence, the public Cloudflare path and physical phones were not exercised (as with multiplayer before).
