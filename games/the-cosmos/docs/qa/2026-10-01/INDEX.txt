  _a_hole.mjs     digging scenario: one bite, then a pit with the bucket, heaps round it, standing in it looking up and round
  _e_deep.mjs     a deep shaft (bucket from the rim, then from inside), heaps, looking up from the bottom, a tunnel walked end to end
  _c_tower.mjs    the portTour views of the tower (outside, stair, cab)
  _c_walk.mjs     WALKED: lobby door, ten flights, into the cab, with the real keys held, and looks round
  _d_drone.mjs    a drone close up on the sun side, beside the Meridian for scale, three together
  _g_gaps.mjs     the gap audit: a dug scene (pit, widened walls, tunnel, ramp, second pit, heaps) drawn on magenta from ~1,650 views;
                  a pure-magenta pixel in a downward view is a hole through everything (sky, clouds, the shell)
