# F0 faction style sheet: review (2026-10-03)

Photographed on the Playwright WebKit iPhone profile (393x852, x2) with `node test/styles-browser.mjs`: one `<id>-people.png` (Loft people in
three uniforms) and `<id>-ship.png` (the Meridian in the livery) per look, with the signs, palette and notes above and below them.

What reads well: every faction's ship is recognisable at thumbnail size (Fortis dark wedge-red, Skyward white-orange, Ironclad oxide, Wanderhome teal,
Corsairs black). Signs and emblems are drawn by emblems.js in each faction's lettering.
What is weak: Loft shirts are dark so uniform tints barely show on some people (Fortis, Corsairs); the livery is a hull recolour only (no stripes, numbers or
awnings on the mesh yet: those are for ship builders); the alien card shows the Meridian in dark colours because its real hull is SH17's job; the stencil
and script fonts fall back on devices without them.
Unverified: a real iPhone (only the WebKit profile on Windows).
