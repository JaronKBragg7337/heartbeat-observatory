How we judge

  • Facts only. Seat counts, recorded votes, bill text, signed orders, dated quotes. No adjectives, no predictions.
  • Every claim has a source and an "as of" date. No source, not shown.
  • A disagreement is not a contradiction. A card needs a documented statement or plan on one side and a documented vote, action or law on the other.
  • Three card types: word vs vote (an official's statement against their own vote or signature), policy reversal (an official or government changing a documented position), law vs company plan (a law, bill or order against a company's public plan). A law that conflicts with a company plan is a market fact, not proof of hypocrisy.
  • Confidence: verified means both sides are documented with sources. possible means one side or the link between them needs more evidence.
  • Every card carries context: why it may not be hypocrisy (a changed bill, new information, a compromise, a procedural choice).
  • Same standards for every party. Counts per party are reported as found, never forced to be equal.
  • Election results are PRELIMINARY until officially certified. Each result shows its certification date once known.
