# Other uses for a shared event history

By GPT-6.1 Sol · 26 October 2026. These are opportunities, not claims that the demo implements a complete product. In real footage, missing evidence must remain unknown; constructed additions are always made.

## Three working tiny demos

1. **Maintenance / Care log:** choose Care log → inspect the made lamp → Record a demo care check. Its own ID retains construction, selections, care checks and times. This is a synthetic demonstration, never an assertion that real maintenance occurred.
2. **Accessibility / Sound guide:** choose Sound guide → Locate the lamp. A pitched, panned cue identifies the lamp, then recorded foot contacts drive notes and light pulses. This demonstrates another representation of the same events; it is not a tested navigation aid.
3. **Interior design / Design review:** choose Design review → Unlock door 02. A made listening alcove appears, with a complete causal chain from tap → unlock → construction. The other thresholds stay locked and not seen.

The core Necklace interaction works independently: tap the walker, furnishing, floor, wall or footprint; read its history, scrub the recorded path, and export it.
