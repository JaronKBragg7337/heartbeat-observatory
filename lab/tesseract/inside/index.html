Tesseract Inside

You stand inside a tesseract, in one of its eight rooms. Every face of your room is an open doorway into a neighbouring room with its own depth and floor, and the eighth room wraps around everything. When the tesseract turns through the fourth direction, your room shrinks away while a neighbour swells around you, until you are standing somewhere else without taking a step. Left alone it rests in a room for a while, then makes one clean move to the next. Drag to look, drag the strip to turn it, walk up to a doorway to see in.
