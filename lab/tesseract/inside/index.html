Tesseract Inside

You stand at ground zero inside a tesseract. Your space is a slice through it, so the room around you is bounded by its cells: every wall, the floor and the ceiling is a door into another room. The tesseract turns through the fourth direction and the rooms slide through you. Drag to look, drag the strip to turn it.
