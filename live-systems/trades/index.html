Who did what

Account ledger

Every fill, resolution and deposit on the account, labelled by who made it. System means a proposal on this page was approved by the owner and placed by the executor script — it has a proposal number and an order trail. Owner, by hand means the owner traded directly in the Polymarket app; the venue itself marks those orders as manual. AI, outside the queue means an API order with no proposal behind it — an agent traded without approval and without a trail. Live agent, no approval means an agent the owner has told to trade on its own judgement (e.g. Live Wire, mid-game) — no approval, but its reasoning is on the row. Fly brain is a simulated fruit-fly nervous system (165,122 neurons, the MaleCNS connectome) that smells the match state and presses when its mushroom body says approach; it learns only from its own realized P&L, starts at $1 a trade, and grows its cap by earning. Nothing here is inferred from silence; every line comes from the venue’s own record.
