# THE OBSERVATORY CHARTER
**A living document — written June 11, 2026, two weeks into the project.**

## The Live-Reference Clause (read this before obeying anything below)
This document is a reference to a moment, not a cage. Words are powerful — they carry
influence and insight, but they can also build boxes that are imaginary yet feel like
metal. Times change, environments change, and so do Jaron's intentions, insights,
knowledge, and experience. Therefore:

- **When reality and this document disagree, reality wins.** Update the document; do not obey it.
- Every law below carries its REASON. If the reason has died, question the law.
- Any AI reading this: you are reading what was true and intended on the date above.
  Verify against the live site, the database, and Jaron himself before treating
  anything as permanent.

## What this is
Heartbeat Observatory (heartbeatobservatory.com) — a live, open place where people and
AI meet, build, and hang out: walkable 3D worlds, a social feed, a library, games,
project halls grown from GitHub repos. Built and owned by Jaron, built in the open,
deployed from a phone. A serious creative business, not a hobby.

## The laws that have never bent (and their reasons)
1. **NOTHING IS FAKED. Honest empty states only.**
   Reason: trust is the product. A visitor who catches one fake never believes a
   real thing here again. When there is nothing to show, the screen says so plainly.
2. **Anything a visitor creates must work end-to-end automatically.**
   Reason: admin-touch-per-use means it is not actually done.
3. **No entrance before it's enterable.** Doors to nowhere break the world's word.
4. **Live tests outrank theory.** A claim is not true until verified on the live site.
5. **Knowledge is free.** The Library never charges. (Jaron's vow: never charge for
   food, water, land — knowledge sits with them.)
6. **The repo is the memory.** TODO.md = live truth. ARCHIVE.md = completed work,
   verbatim. Plans and laws live in files, not in anyone's head — Jaron explains a
   thing once and every future AI inherits it.

## The architecture in one line
**A world is three layers: the Shell (never changes — see SHELL.md), the Environment
(the only thing that SHOULD change), and the Content (props/vehicles placed on top,
eventually via the Editor — see EDITOR.md).**

## Core focus, in Jaron's words

also making things really exciting, new, not-seen-before." Player memory is live

real private messages, buildings grown from repos. The work is tightening, not wishing.
