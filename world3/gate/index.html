Full screen, on phone or computer. Your character and your messages come with you from the other worlds.

What is actually here

  • Every house is solved at runtime against a real dimension code book, then validated — room area, hall width, stair fit, reachability from the front door.
  • The residents wake, walk to work or school, run errands and go home. Dogs come along on errands; cats hold the yard.
  • The town grows itself. Demand for housing raises a new house on a real lot, built by residents whose labour is actually counted.
  • You can take a job seat from the resident who holds it — and lose it back to them if you stop showing up.

The seams are part of the design

The town itself is identical for everyone: it is grown from one fixed seed, so the buildings, the residents and their routines are the same place for every visitor. Live players appear and move in real time.

Buildings raised after founding are still saved per browser while the shared record is being wired, so someone who has been away longer may see a slightly smaller town than you do. That is the next piece, not a mystery.
