World 2

The second world of Heartbeat Observatory.

Being built in the open. A downtown on a river: real streets with sidewalks, crosswalks, lamps, signals and traffic, storefronts and the courthouse green. Doors with a green mat are real: the Grand Palace, the Arcade and the Library open into their halls; Social Hall, Projects, WHB News and Union Station (trains to Town Square and Ashgrove) carry you to the rest of the Observatory. Eight open lots wait for residents — claim one with a GitHub repo and it becomes your building. Shops, jobs and the news studio come in later builds. Multiplayer is live.
