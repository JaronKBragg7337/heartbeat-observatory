Worlds Lab STAGED · NOT YET ON THE TOWN MAP

New walkable worlds for the Observatory, built in the open. Everything below runs right now on a phone or a computer — rideable coasters, trains, helicopter tours, a rocket launch, and movie theaters playing real, legally free films.

Clear by design. Lab worlds are solo previews: no shared presence and no accounts yet. Each world carries its current status on the way in. They live in new folders only; not one line of the live town's code was touched. When a world earns promotion, the plug-in checklist in worlds-lab/README.md wires it into the real map.

What's in the kit (lib/v1 — frozen)

kit.js The world chassis: renderer, day/night, World-1 movement feel, touch + keyboard parity, colliders, doors, interiors, and the ride system.
textures.js 20+ procedural canvas textures — brick, planks, sand, snow, water, neon, facades, marquees, posters. Zero downloaded art.
props.js 25+ code-built props: lamps, benches, trees, fountains, carts, stalls, torches, snowmen, coral, arcade cabinets…
buildings.js Houses (7 styles), shops, lit towers, lighthouse, castle keep, observatory, control tower, barn, walk-in room shells.
vehicles.js Cars, taxis, buses, trucks, golf carts, boats — parked, patrolling, or rideable.
rails.js Track splines, stations, steam trains you can board.
coaster.js A full roller coaster builder with energy-based speed and support pillars.
rides.js Ferris wheel, carousel, sky swings — all rideable.
aircraft.js Helicopters with scripted tours, hot air balloons, blimps.
space.js Rockets with launch rides, UFOs, shuttle displays, dishes, moon buggies.
cinema.js Walk-in theaters with real video on the screen and the license credit on the wall.
layouts.js Terrains (hills, island, canyon, dunes, snow, craters) and street/plaza/boardwalk layouts.
comingsoon.js The preview gate kit, ready for any future world.
Every theater reel streams from its original free source — Blender Foundation open movies (CC BY, credited in-world), Internet Archive public-domain features, and NASA public-domain footage.
