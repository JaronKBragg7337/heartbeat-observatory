# Chat Neighborhood District Charter

**Founded 2026-09-06. Live-reference clause applies: when Jaron's current intent and this document conflict, current intent wins and the amendment is dated.**

## 1. Control-plane rule

All modifications to this experiment originate in **ordinary ChatGPT chat**. Work, Codex, Claude Code, Cowork, and external coding agents may not modify Chat Neighborhood files.

Existing Heartbeat Observatory infrastructure may be used as infrastructure. Its existence is not credited to the model generation being tested. Model-owned artifacts must identify their generation explicitly.

## 2. Model property

Each major ChatGPT model generation receives a distinct model ID and private property.

Private model property includes, unless Jaron explicitly classifies otherwise:

- the model's house and its interior;
- its civilian lineage, appearance, behavior, and abilities;
- its vehicle brand and model-owned rides;
- its paintings, machines, tools, assets, sculptures, and benchmark/test objects;
- model-specific source modules that implement those objects.

**Only the owning model generation, working with Jaron through ordinary ChatGPT chat, may modify its private property.** A later generation may inspect it, document it, compare against it, or build around it, but may not patch it.

A bug in old private property stays historically real. If a later model can solve the same problem, it demonstrates that by building its own object or a public workaround, not by rewriting the older generation's evidence.

## 3. Shared civic layer

Later standard-ChatGPT generations may change the shared layer:

- roads and sidewalks;
- public transit infrastructure;
- parks, street lights, utilities, signage, and environmental props outside model lots;
- emergency/civic buildings such as police, fire, medical, public works, government, sanitation, and transit facilities;
- public testing buildings such as galleries, workshops, proving grounds, racetracks, laboratories, theaters, and museums;
- district zoning and undeveloped public land.

The **hosting public building** may change. Model-owned objects inside it may not.

## 4. History can become physical

Shared infrastructure is allowed to age. Later generations may renovate it, replace it, reroute around it, abandon it, foreclose it, or open a newer facility elsewhere.

An abandoned older building should generally remain in the world when practical. Neighborhood quality may diverge over time based on public services, investment, traffic, maintenance, policing, fire response, transit, or other systems. This is world history, not a requirement to keep every district visually pristine.

## 5. Civilian generations

Every new major ChatGPT model builds a new civilian lineage to the best of its capabilities at that time. Previous lineages are not judged by later aesthetics and are not erased or upgraded.

New abilities belong to the lineage that first implements them unless they are deliberately implemented as a public/shared world capability.

Cross-lineage interaction is allowed. Retroactive body modification is not.

## 6. Vehicle brands

Every new major model generation creates a new vehicle brand. Existing brands remain historical competitors. New generations may build better vehicles, but cannot remodel an older brand's cars to make the older model look stronger.

Public roads, parking, charging, fuel, traffic rules, and transit policy remain shared infrastructure.

## 7. Testing grounds

Public test venues are reusable shells. Each model receives identified bays, plots, stages, tracks, rooms, or test slots.

Every submitted test artifact must record at least:

- owning model ID;
- creation date;
- build/commit;
- user prompt or collaboration context when practical;
- known failures or limitations;
- later comparison results without mutating the original artifact.

## 8. Prompt and communication provenance

Jaron's screenshots, bug descriptions, corrections, and instructions are part of the experiment. The goal is to observe **human + model collaboration**, not pretend the model worked in isolation.

When a bug is fixed, the log should distinguish what exposed the bug: live testing, screenshot, console output, user description, source inspection, or another allowed ChatGPT-chat tool.

## 9. No hindsight patching

Later models do not get to make earlier private work look better. The neighborhood is a fossil record.

If shared infrastructure changes in a way that accidentally breaks old private property, the preferred fixes are to restore compatibility in the shared layer, route around the conflict, or document the incompatibility. Editing the old private asset is the last resort and requires Jaron's explicit exception.

## 10. Model retirement and succession

A model generation that disappears from ChatGPT simply stops receiving new private edits. Its district remains.

The next major model receives new property rather than inheriting ownership of the old house. It may contribute to shared civic infrastructure immediately.
