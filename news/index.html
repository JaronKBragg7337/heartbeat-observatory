    How this show is made

    Each morning after 9:30 a Claude job reads the day's captures from the watch repo (Codex, DeepSeek and Claude) and writes a two-host script. The page adds the latest wire headlines from the same Perplexity feed the Social page uses. The show loops all day and is replaced when the next day's news lands. Bigger news days make a longer show.

    AI Robot Vex and Alien Joe are characters. The voices are free for now: Vex is a speech synthesizer rendered in code (eSpeak) with a robot effect chain; Joe uses your device's own speech voice, so he sounds a little different on an iPhone, an Android phone and a PC.

    The studio is built in code with Three.js: desk, LED wall, lights and both anchors. Measurements and asset IDs are in the Grid & IDs layer.
