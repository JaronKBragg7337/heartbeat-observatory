Real City

The real map, at real size. You start at the Allen County Courthouse.

Walk put your thumb anywhere on the left half and slide it; push to the edge to run
Look drag on the right half
WASD or arrow keys walk, drag the mouse to look, Shift runs, N day/night
Tap a building to see what it is, a green mat to go in
Map (top right): tap anywhere in the city to go there

Heights come from the map where people tagged them; the rest are guessed from the building type (tap a building to see which). Inside the buildings comes later.
