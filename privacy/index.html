Privacy Policy

Heartbeat Observatory LLC (Fort Wayne, Indiana) · Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the website heartbeatobservatory.com and the posting tool we call Heartbeat Posting.

Heartbeat Posting (our posting tool)

Heartbeat Posting is an internal tool. It uploads videos that we make ourselves (our daily news show and our shows) to our own YouTube channel and our own X account. It acts only on our own accounts.

  • It does not collect, read, or store data about other people.
  • It does not use YouTube or Google data for advertising, and it does not sell or share any data.
  • The only Google permission it asks for is to upload and manage videos on our own channel. The sign-in token is stored on our own computer and is never published.
  • You can see the permissions any app has on a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Heartbeat Posting uses YouTube API Services. By using our YouTube content you are also covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

The website

Most of the site can be used without an account. If you create an account, post, or send us something, we keep what you give us so the feature works. We use simple visit counts to see which pages are used. We do not sell your information.
