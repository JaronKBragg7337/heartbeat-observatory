THE IDEA BEHIND THE SCENE

Nature doesn’t need
an invitation.

It starts at the edges. Grass grows tall; leaves and soil collect in the places we used to clear. Water finds openings in the pavement. Over time, plants find places to take root.

Researchers studying abandoned roads in British Columbia found that plant cover changed with time and differed between asphalt and gravel. This is one possible Indiana story, made to explore—not a forecast for every road.
